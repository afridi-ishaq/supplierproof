# 00 — Product Vision & Founder’s Charter

**Product:** SupplierProof (working name)  
**Positioning:** B2B Supply Chain Evidence and Intelligence Platform  
**Tagline:** Every supplier. Every claim. Backed by evidence.  
**Source:** Product Vision & Founder’s Charter v0.2 (18 November 2026)

## Executive summary
SupplierProof helps manufacturers, exporters, suppliers and buyers collect, organize, connect, review and selectively share evidence about suppliers, sites, materials, products and production batches.

The central workflow turns a buyer request into structured evidence requirements, links those requirements to relevant supply-chain entities and records, identifies gaps and exceptions, supports human review, and produces a controlled, versioned evidence pack.

The long-term ambition is reusable evidence infrastructure—not a generic CRM, document drive or supplier directory.

## Problem hypotheses
- Supplier information and supporting records may be fragmented across email, spreadsheets, cloud folders, PDFs and messaging apps.
- Buyers may repeatedly request similar evidence, causing duplicated collection and review work.
- Documents may be missing, expired, incomplete, inconsistent or linked to the wrong supplier, site, material, product or batch.
- Businesses may lack a clear view of which upstream entities and transactions are affected by a missing or outdated record.
- Smaller exporters and suppliers may find broad enterprise platforms difficult or costly to adopt.

These are hypotheses to validate with customers, not established market facts.

## Initial beachhead hypothesis
Start discovery with Pakistani textile and textile-related exporters/manufacturers serving international buyers, including European buyers. This is a discovery focus, not a permanent industry limitation. Keep the underlying domain model sector-flexible.

## Product principles
1. Evidence must be connected to the entity and scope it describes.
2. Preserve original files and immutable version history.
3. Separate human review from provenance, source verification, integrity and recorded validity.
4. Make supplier-controlled reuse possible without exposing the whole vault.
5. Sharing is explicit, scoped and revocable.
6. AI is optional, advisory and subject to human review.
7. Security, tenant isolation and auditability are foundational.
8. Prefer a narrow validated workflow over broad feature accumulation.

## Differentiation hypotheses
- Reusable Evidence Network.
- Evidence Relationship Intelligence across supplier, site, material, product, batch and transaction.
- Transparent Trust & Provenance.
- Buyer-Ready Evidence Passport.

These are hypotheses, not claims of uniqueness. Validate them through competitor research and real pilots.

## Non-goals and prohibited claims
SupplierProof does not automatically prove supplier legitimacy, document authenticity, legal compliance, ethical performance or product quality. Do not market the platform as “guaranteed compliant”, “fraud-proof”, or “authentic by AI” without a defined, substantiated basis.

## Initial success signals to investigate
- Time required to complete a buyer evidence request.
- Number of follow-up requests and missing/expired items.
- Evidence reuse across multiple requests.
- Supplier activation and buyer acceptance of evidence packs.
- Reviewer understanding of trust labels.
- Willingness to pay and repeat usage.

## Open validation questions
- Which buyer requests are frequent and costly enough to justify a dedicated workflow?
- Who owns the budget: exporter, manufacturer, buyer or another party?
- Which records are reusable and which must be transaction-specific?
- What evidence scope and permissions do buyers expect?
- What onboarding burden is acceptable to small and mid-sized suppliers?
